About PCL

We do the work ourselves, and we stay accountable for it

PCL is an independent consultancy founded on a simple idea: senior people, clear scopes, and outcomes you can measure.

Our story

PCL started when a small team of operators and technologists noticed how often good businesses suffer from bad execution — strategy that never reached the shop floor, software bought but never adopted, and consultants who wrote reports nobody could use.

We built the firm we wanted to hire: one that stays involved until results show up, hands over capability, and treats trust as the only reputation that matters.
